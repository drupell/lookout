# Delaware EV Incentives

## Delaware Clean Vehicle Rebate Program

The Delaware Clean Transportation Incentive Program provides rebates for the purchase or lease of new qualified plug-in electric vehicles (PEVs).

### Rebate Amounts
- **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs):** Up to $2,500 rebate
- **Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs):** Up to $1,000 rebate

### Eligibility Requirements
- Vehicle must be new (not used)
- Vehicle must be purchased or leased from a Delaware-licensed dealer
- Applicant must be a Delaware resident
- Vehicle MSRP must not exceed $60,000
- Applicant's adjusted gross income must not exceed $150,000 (single) or $200,000 (joint)
- Limited to one rebate per household per calendar year

### How to Apply
- Submit application within 90 days of vehicle purchase or lease
- Applications processed on a first-come, first-served basis
- Program is subject to available funding

## Delaware Utility EV Programs

### Delmarva Power
- Time-of-use rate available for EV owners
- Off-peak charging rates significantly lower
- Smart charger rebate programs available periodically

### Delaware Electric Cooperative
- Special EV rate for residential members
- Level 2 charger installation rebate up to $500

## Delaware EV Registration
- EVs are subject to an additional annual registration fee of $110
- This fee offsets lost gasoline tax revenue
- Standard registration fees also apply
