# Federal EV Tax Credits — Clean Vehicle Credit (IRC Section 30D)

## New Clean Vehicle Credit

### Credit Amount
- Up to $7,500 for qualifying new electric vehicles
- Credit is composed of two components:
  - $3,750 for meeting critical mineral requirements
  - $3,750 for meeting battery component requirements
- A vehicle may qualify for $3,750, $7,500, or $0 depending on compliance

### Vehicle Eligibility Requirements
- Must be a new, qualified plug-in electric drive motor vehicle
- Must have a battery capacity of at least 7 kWh
- Must be manufactured by a qualified manufacturer
- Final assembly must occur in North America (US, Canada, or Mexico)
- Vehicle identification number (VIN) can be checked at fueleconomy.gov

### MSRP Limits (2024-2025)
- Vans: $80,000
- SUVs: $80,000
- Pickup trucks: $80,000
- Sedans / other vehicles: $55,000

### Income Limits (Modified Adjusted Gross Income)
- Single filers: $150,000
- Head of household: $225,000
- Joint filers: $300,000
- Based on the lesser of current year or prior year MAGI

### How to Claim
- Starting 2024, credit can be transferred to the dealer at point of sale
- Dealer transfers reduce purchase price immediately
- Alternatively, claim on Form 8936 with annual tax return

## Used Clean Vehicle Credit (IRC Section 25E)

### Credit Amount
- Up to $4,000 or 30% of the sale price, whichever is less

### Eligibility Requirements
- Vehicle must be at least 2 model years old
- Sale price must be $25,000 or less
- Must be purchased from a licensed dealer
- Vehicle must not have previously generated a used vehicle credit

### Income Limits
- Single filers: $75,000
- Head of household: $112,500
- Joint filers: $150,000
